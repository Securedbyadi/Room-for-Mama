# Room for Mama: bookings for a gentle routine coach

## The business
A real practice run from Lahore: gentle routine coaching for mothers of babies and toddlers (0–3) in Pakistan and anywhere English is spoken. The coach is a mother of a baby herself: one nap ahead, not an expert.
- She works in nap-sized windows. Mothers book at 2–4 a.m., in any time zone.
- Calls move all the time, on both sides. Free first calls get ghosted.
Goal: a 3 a.m. "can I book?" becomes a booked hello call in a minute; one tap books Make Room; moving costs nobody a message; the coach sees only exceptions.

## Feel and words
- A friend making you a cup of tea, never a clinic. Nothing asks more than a tap.
- The coach says "I", the mother is "you", "we" only means the two of them. Short, plain, warm sentences, sentence case, curly apostrophes. No exclamation marks, no emoji, never "should", "mistake" or "fix".
- Say half hour or call, hello call, mother or mama, move, after baby, plan. Never session, appointment, consultation, client, patient, user, reschedule, postpartum, programme, therapy, self-care, journey, burnout, overwhelm.

## Rules (owner-editable settings)
Coach zone Asia/Karachi. Every time shows in the mother’s zone (detected, with a Change link), labelled.
Coach windows Mon–Fri 14:00–17:00 and 21:00–23:00; max 3 calls a day; 10 min buffer; calls start on :00 or :30. Video: her own Google Meet link.
- Hello call: 20 min, free.
- Make Room: 4 half hours, one a week. PKR 12,000 if her zone is Asia/Karachi, else US$120; founding price for the first 10 mothers PKR 8,000 / US$80.
Notice: 6 h for new calls, 1 h for moves; up to 6 weeks ahead.
Keep my spot: a link 24 h before each hello call (none if booked later). Not tapped by 3 h before: the time goes to the first on the waitlist; nobody waiting, it stays.
Payment: times are held 48 h; the hold screen shows payment details (bank, Raast, JazzCash, Wise); she taps "I’ve paid" with a reference; the coach confirms in one tap; unpaid holds release.
Baby’s up: each side can move a call, free, twice per call, until 10 min after the start.
Cancelling: full refund before the first call; after that, the rest can pause up to 8 weeks. The first missed Make Room call is put back.
Clock changes: keep the mother’s local time if it still fits both windows; otherwise keep the coach’s time and tell her the new time when she books, or 7 days ahead.

## Mother’s flow (mobile-first, no account)
1. A website: home, what I offer, who it’s for, prices, FAQ. "Book a free hello call" opens 4 tap questions; all fit: booking; any no: a kind not-a-fit page. Answers aren’t stored.
2. "When do you usually get a quiet moment?" in her words, any language (helper: "Write it your way, in any language. Only days, times and your city are kept."). Lovable AI extracts only those; store them, not her words. Offer the 3 earliest open times, one per day, inside both windows, in her time. None fit: "No times match yet. Join the waitlist and I’ll email you when one opens."
3. Tap a time, add first name, email, optional phone, then Book → "You’re in" → confirmation email and calendar invite with the video link. The coach gets the same invite for every booking and move.
4. After the hello call, if offered: the Make Room page shows all 4 calls at one weekly time inside both windows, in her time, with clock-change notes. "Book all four" → held → paid → confirmed → invites.
5. 30 min before each call: a reminder with Baby’s up. One tap shows up to 3 new times (same rule, first selected); "Move my call" moves it and updates the invites.
6. After each call: a thank-you email with the coach’s one small step. Emails from hello@roomformama.com via Lovable Cloud: one branded template, one action each.
7. Manage link (token, no login): calls, move, pay, cancel. A privacy page: what is kept, why, how to delete it.

## Coach app (login)
- Tabs: Today, Mothers, Rules, Given back. Today: her calls in her time, each mother’s local time alongside.
- Her own Baby’s up on any call: one tap sends that mother 3 new times. She types nothing.
- Needs you: exceptions only (payment checks, a third move, a missed call), one tap each.
- After a hello call: "Offer Make Room" or "Not a fit" (a kind note she writes once in settings). After any call: one line for the small step.
- Mothers: calls, payments, private notes, one-tap "Share helplines".
- Time given back: week, month and all-time totals, labelled "estimated", plus a feed.
- "Run today’s automations now".

## Automations
A job every 5 min sends reminders and Keep my spot links, releases spots and ends expired holds; a 07:00 Lahore job sends the coach’s digest. Each action writes automation_log (type, mother, minutes_saved). Minutes (editable): booked 20, Keep my spot 10, offer 5, 4 calls 25, payment 10, reminder 5, move 10, clock notice 10, re-offer 10, digest 15.

## Safety (non-negotiable)
Friendly support, not medical care: she coaches the mother’s own day, never the baby’s sleep, feeding or health; no diagnosis, questionnaires, mood tracking or scores. Every booking screen, email and the footer end with SafetyNote: "Room for Mama is friendly support, not medical care. If you feel low most days, please talk to your doctor or a helpline. If you have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, call a helpline or your local emergency number now." Then her country’s lines (owner-editable table), seeded:
- Pakistan: Umang 0311 7786264 (24 h); emergency 1122
- UK: Samaritans 116 123 (24 h); PANDAS WhatsApp 07903 508334 (weekdays 9–5); emergency 999

- Saudi Arabia: National Center for Mental Health Promotion 920033360 (8 am–8 pm); emergency 911
- US: National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262 (24 h, call/text); 988; emergency 911
- Canada: 988 (24 h, call/text); emergency 911
- Anywhere else: "Call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline at findahelpline.com."
Calendar titles and email subjects: "Half hour with Room for Mama" (hello calls: "Hello call with Room for Mama"). Payment references: RM and a number. Nothing personal in any notification.

## Integrity and security
- No double-booking: a database constraint on the coach’s calendar.
- RLS on every table. Visitors never read tables; mother actions go through edge functions that check the manage token (random, 32+ chars).
- Private notes in their own table, coach role only.
- Rate limit: 5 bookings per email, 20 per IP, per hour.
