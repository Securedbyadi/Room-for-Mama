# Form layout: drawing on the left, form on the right

Decision, 30 September 2026 (plan: keep-the-split-form-layout-with-a-light-polish-pass).

The fit-check and booking forms keep the split layout on desktop: the drawing sits on the left on a sunk half, and the one-column form (max 480 px) sits on the right. The form is never stretched to the middle of the page.

- The drawing on the left is capped at about 240 px wide on desktop, with the butter glow hugging it (about 280 px), so the form gets breathing room.
- The drawing sits vertically centred in its half on tall steps.
- On tablets and phones the drawing sits above the question at 160 px, as before.
- Every step keeps its assigned drawings; no white background behind drawings; all other behaviour unchanged.

Why: a centred form on a wide screen reads like a checkout page and loses the friendly feel; the approved references and the mother-flow plan both specify this split.
